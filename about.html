About CineFlix

Discover your next story.

CineFlix is an independent movie and entertainment discovery website created by Ronney Keya. It brings movies, TV shows, and anime together so visitors can browse titles, explore available details and trailers, and keep track of favorites.

What CineFlix offers

Browse popular and trending titles, search the catalog, and explore movie, television, and anime collections. Catalog details and availability may depend on third-party services and can change over time.

Sign in and create an account

Create a CineFlix account or sign in to use account features such as saving titles to your list and keeping track of your watch history. These features make it easier to pick up where you left off and keep your discoveries together.

Free to explore, wherever you are

CineFlix is free to use, so you can explore the site from anywhere with an internet connection. CineFlix is a discovery platform, not a streaming service; whether and where a title can be streamed depends on the services that offer it and your location.

Independent project

CineFlix is an independent discovery project and is not an official streaming service or affiliated with film studios, distributors, or the owners of the titles displayed. Names, artwork, and other title information belong to their respective owners.
